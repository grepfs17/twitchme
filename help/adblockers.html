How to whitelist Twitch channels in your ad blocker

Whitelisting Twitch channels allows the streamer to earn ad revenue. Add the filter rule below to your ad blocker to disable blocking on a specific channel.

@@||twitch.tv/CHANNEL_NAME$document

Replace CHANNEL_NAME with the streamer's username (e.g. mushu).

  1. Click the uBlock Origin icon in your toolbar
  2. Click the gears icon (Open the dashboard)
  3. Go to the My filters tab
  4. Add the filter rule on a new line:
    @@||twitch.tv/mushu$document
  5. Click Apply changes
  6. Reload the Twitch stream page

AdBlock Plus

  1. Click the AdBlock Plus icon in your toolbar
  2. Click Options (gear icon)
  3. Go to the Advanced tab
  4. Scroll down to Custom filters
  5. Click Add filter and enter:
    @@||twitch.tv/mushu$document
  6. Reload the Twitch stream page

AdGuard

  1. Click the AdGuard icon in your toolbar
  2. Click the gear icon (Settings)
  3. Go to Allowlist in the left menu
  4. Add the filter rule:
    @@||twitch.tv/mushu$document
  5. Click Add
  6. Reload the Twitch stream page

AdBlock

  1. Click the AdBlock icon in your toolbar
  2. Click Options
  3. Go to the Customize tab
  4. Scroll to Stop blocking ads
  5. Enter the filter rule:
    @@||twitch.tv/mushu$document
  6. Click Add
  7. Reload the Twitch stream page

After adding the filter, reload the stream page. The ad blocker will no longer block ads on that specific channel.
